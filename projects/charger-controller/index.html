FSAE Charger Controller

A redesigned charger controller for McMaster Formula Electric’s accumulator charger, built to FSAE rules with integrated safety logic and power converters while cutting BOM cost by 15%.

Context

McMaster Formula Electric · High Voltage Electronics Engineer

What I did

  • Redesigned the Charger Controller per FSAE rules, from requirements through manufacturing and testing.
  • Integrated safety logic circuits and power converters, reducing BOM cost by 15% through design optimization.
  • Built hardware test fixtures and conducted impedance testing and thermal simulations on the charger hardware.
  • Performed hardware debug and electrical design verification across fail-safe operating states.
  • Produced test plans, technical reports, and fabrication and assembly documentation for review.
