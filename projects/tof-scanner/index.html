ToF Spatial Scanner

A stepper-driven VL53L1X time-of-flight scanner streaming over UART to MATLAB for real-time 3D point-cloud reconstruction.

Stack

MSP432E401Y · C · Stepper Motor Control · I2C/UART · MATLAB

What I did

  • Developed C firmware synchronizing a stepper motor with VL53L1X time-of-flight sensor readings via I2C.
  • Streamed scan data over UART to MATLAB for real-time 3D point-cloud reconstruction.
  • Characterized scan reliability through analysis of motor timing, sensor quantization, and UART throughput.
